3. Result Discussion
From the histogram and statistical results, we can draw the following conclusions:
1. The distribution of DALYs across countries in 2019 is significantly right-skewed: most countries have relatively low DALYs values, while only a small number of countries have extremely high disease burden.
2. The mean DALYs value is higher than the median, which further confirms the right-skewed feature of the distribution.
3. This result is in line with expectations: countries with large populations and relatively underdeveloped public health systems tend to have higher DALYs, while most small and high-income developed countries have lower overall disease burden.
